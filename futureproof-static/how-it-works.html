HOW IT WORKS

From “I’m not sure” to a clear next step.

FUTUREPROOF is designed to make business compliance easier to understand without turning the website into a complicated portal.

01

Choose your path

Already registered? Start the compliance assessment. Not registered? Go straight to registration support.

02

Tell us about your business

Select your industry or sector, state of operation, LGA, employee range and turnover range from guided dropdown lists.

03

Review your status

Receive an informational FUTUREPROOF score and see which areas appear complete, need attention, or require action.

04

Choose how to get help

Call the team, continue on WhatsApp or submit a support ticket directly to email.

NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SCORE

A practical assessment, not a regulatory verdict.

Your FUTUREPROOF Score is based on the information you provide. It is designed to help you identify possible areas for review and is not an official government compliance rating, legal opinion or tax opinion.
